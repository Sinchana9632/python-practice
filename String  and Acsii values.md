
- **0 to 31:** Control characters (like Enter/Newline `\n` or Tab `\t`)
    
- **32 to 47:** Special characters (`!`, `"`, `#`, `$`, space, etc.)
    
- **48 to 57:** Digits (`'0'` to `'9'`)
    
- **58 to 64:** Special characters (`:`, `;`, `<`, `=`, `>`, `?`, `@`)
    
- **65 to 90:** Uppercase letters (`'A'` to `'Z'`)
    
- **91 to 96:** Special characters (`[`, `\`, `]`, `^`, `_`, `` ` ``)
    
- **97 to 122:** Lowercase letters (`'a'` to `'z'`)
    
- **123 to 127:** Special characters (`{`, `|`, `}`, `~`)
